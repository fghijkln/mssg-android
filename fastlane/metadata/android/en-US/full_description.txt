WebWeave is a static site generator that runs entirely on your phone — no desktop computer needed.

Describe the site you want in one sentence and the built-in AI assistant generates it; preview it on the phone, then export a ZIP or deploy to Cloudflare Pages.

Features:
* AI site builder and AI writing/coding assistants (requires your own OpenAI-compatible API key)
* Plugin system with shortcodes
* Markdown editor, themes, on-device site search
* Backup/restore, Cloudflare Pages deployment

WebWeave is free software, released under the MIT license. Cloudflare deployment and the AI features are optional and configured by the user; the core works fully offline.
